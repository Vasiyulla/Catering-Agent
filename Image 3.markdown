## Brand & Style

The visual identity marries the ancestral heritage of royal Mughlai and Punjabi banquet traditions with Mayfair and Knightsbridge gastronomic sophistication. It caters to discerning private hosts, corporate banquet organizers, and wedding parties seeking authentic heritage dining paired with British Halal certified culinary execution.

The interface embodies **Warm Regal Editorial Minimalism**:
- Majestic, architectural framing using rich botanical depths and warm spice accents.
- Luxurious negative space balanced with tailored gold filigree highlights and micro-details.
- Deep, appetizing materiality: translucent amber gradients, warm cream parchment backdrops, and deep forest-tinted obsidian foundations.
- Restrained, unhurried interactions that convey prestige, precision, and heartfelt hospitality.

## Colors

The color system draws from royal Indian court dining and timeless British luxury institutions:

- **Primary (`#0C2B22` - Royal Emerald Green):** The commanding anchor color. Represents prestige, botanical purity, and imperial banquet halls. Used for primary navigation, authoritative typography, and luxury container surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#D9531E` - Saffron Spice, hover `#BF4413`):** The operational accent. Evokes slow-roasted saffron, tandoor heat, and culinary passion. Reserved for key calls-to-action, active selection rings, and booking catalysts.
- **Tertiary (`#C89D42` - Regal Warm Gold):** The heritage embellishment. Used for delicate borders, Halal certification insignias, award accents, and bespoke dietary badges.
- **Neutral Dark (`#111317` - Rich Obsidian Dark):** High-contrast text, deep modal scrims, and sharp luxury footer planes.
- **Background (`#FAF8F5` - Warm Cream):** Soft linen-inspired base ensuring the interface feels inviting, tactile, and hospitable rather than sterile.
- **Surface (`#FFFFFF` - Crisp White Surface):** Elevated cards, menu panels, and intake forms to provide pristine contrast against the warm cream canvas.

## Typography

The type scale combines architectural geometry with warm readability:

- **Outfit (Display & Titles):** Employs balanced proportions and clean geometric precision. Used for course titles, grand tier pricing, and banquet narratives. Capitalized tracking (`label-caps`) delivers an editorial, high-society tone for course labels, allergens, and Halal authenticity markers.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body & Operational Interfaces):** Offers humanist curves and open apertures. Keeps long ingredient manifests, dietary requirements, and banquet logistics comfortable to scan.
- **Pricing & Grand Totals:** Rendered exclusively in `Outfit` (`price-callout`), anchoring guest minimums and bespoke package quotes with confidence.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column responsive grid structures banquet tiers, menu selections, and client consultations:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and `3rem` (48px) margins. Maximum container width capped at `1360px` to maintain focused culinary storytelling.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` (20px) gutters, `2rem` (32px) margins. Menu builder panels transition into split column view.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, `1.25rem` (20px) outer margins. Sticky culinary consultation actions dock to bottom sheet areas.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Course lists and tasting notes use `space-md` gaps. Sections breathing space scales between `space-2xl` and `space-3xl` to preserve the relaxed air of a fine dining salon.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through tailored light diffusion and low-contrast metallic rimming rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Floor):** Base canvas in `#FAF8F5` (Warm Cream).
- **Level 1 (Menu Cards, Dish Tiles):** Background `#FFFFFF` with a subtle composite shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(12, 43, 34, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(17, 19, 23, 0.03)`, encased in a delicate border of `1px solid rgba(200, 157, 66, 0.22)` (Regal Warm Gold).
- **Level 2 (Active Selections, Popovers):** Background `#FFFFFF`, shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(12, 43, 34, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(17, 19, 23, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Grand Tasting Modals & Tasting Sheets):** Shadow: `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(12, 43, 34, 0.18)`, overlaid upon a velvet emerald scrim: `rgba(12, 43, 34, 0.65)` with an `8px` backdrop blur.
- **Gold Accent Sheen:** Selected packages and royal recommendations receive a micro-stroke overlay `box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(200, 157, 66, 0.45)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces **Soft Structured Precision** (`roundedness: 1`):

- **Standard Base Radii (0.25rem / 4px):** Form fields, interactive course counters, and table tags evoke the bespoke trim of tailored menus.
- **Card & Component Radii (0.5rem / 8px):** Tasting cards, booking calendars, and course item containers.
- **Signature Exceptions:** Micro-badges (Halal certification, dietary allergens) and pill-shaped guest count toggles leverage `9999px` full roundness to evoke imperial seal tokens.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Request Banquet Proposal"):** Solid Saffron Spice (`#D9531E`) background, crisp white label (`Outfit`, medium), `4px` radius. Hover state shifts to `#BF4413` with a smooth `200ms ease-out` transition and a soft ambient glow `0 4px 14px rgba(217, 83, 30, 0.28)`.
- **Royal Secondary (e.g., "Curate Bespoke Menu"):** Deep Emerald Green (`#0C2B22`) background with warm gold text (`#C89D42`) and a fine border of `1px solid #C89D42`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background, Royal Emerald text with an animated 1px underline in Regal Warm Gold that expands on hover.

### Menu & Course Cards
- Crisp white ground, bound by `1px solid rgba(200, 157, 66, 0.25)`.
- Photography containers feature an 8:5 golden ratio with a subtle inset shadow to frame Mughlai hand-finished plating.
- Pricing callouts positioned top right using `price-callout` styling in `#0C2B22`.

### Chips & Dietary Badges
- **Halal Luxury Seal:** Background `rgba(12, 43, 34, 0.06)`, border `1px solid #C89D42`, text `#0C2B22`, uppercase `label-caps` tracking with a mini gold crescent icon.
- **Interactive Dietary Filters (Jain, Vegan, Nut-Free):** Warm cream pill chips that toggle to solid Emerald Green with white text upon selection.

### Form Inputs & Tasting Calculators
- Base state: Crisp white background, `1px solid rgba(17, 19, 23, 0.15)`, padded with `space-md`.
- Focus state: Border shifts to Saffron Spice (`#D9531E`) with an outer ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(217, 83, 30, 0.12)`.
- Floating labels set in Plus Jakarta Sans with a discreet warm gold accent when active.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Custom square (`4px` radius) or circular controls in `1px solid #C89D42`.
- Checked state transitions to a solid Emerald Green background holding a clean Saffron or Gold checkmark glyph.

### Banquet Course Sequencer (Custom Component)
- Horizontal progress bar connecting culinary stations (Canape Reception, Shahi Dastarkhwan, Sweet Finale). Nodes display gold metallic rings that fill with Emerald Green upon client confirmation.
